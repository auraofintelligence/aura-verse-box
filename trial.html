Proposal pages 1–3

The proposed public introduction

Verse Box would introduce Aura of Intelligence, a personally owned artificial intelligence environment, through demonstrations and assisted personal or community projects. Suitable second-hand equipment would help keep the trial budget low.

Hosts, locations, dates, activities, costs and contact arrangements would be agreed before a public invitation.

Proposal page 22

Preparing and reviewing the trial

A trial on Minjerribah and across Redlands would require hosts, funding, tested equipment and agreed operating responsibilities. First Nations participation and cultural material would follow agreements with the appropriate people. [5, pp. 9-11]

Work to completeProposed outcome
Local programmeAgree the hosts, venues, activities, staffing, contact arrangements and council or community processes involved.
Equipment and budgetInventory reusable equipment; test computers with the intended sensors and applications; price missing components, transport, installation and support.
Station and softwareComplete the flatpack design, stability and access checks; test assembly, capture, calibration, application performance and session handover.
Personal recordsDemonstrate device or browser collection, permissions, export, correction, deletion and recovery, including treatment of public-station session data.
Community connectionsTest conversational contribution recognition with participants and organisers, and connect civic submissions with the responsible receiving channels.
Access and assistanceArrange suitable interfaces, language support, physical access and assisted participation for people without their own device.

Review the results

A review would examine completed tasks, measurement quality, application reliability, privacy, costs and support effort. Participant feedback would cover what helped, what failed and what prevented access. Civic cases would track submissions, responses, decisions and observed outcomes.
