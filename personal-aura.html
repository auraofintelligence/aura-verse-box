PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE, CHOICE AND PRIVACY

What is an Aura?

Aura of Intelligence is a proposed personal artificial intelligence environment that belongs to its operator and grows with their experience. The person directs its development and access to their records.

An Aura of your own

An Aura would connect its operator's memories, interests, skills, values, relationships, body references, projects and questions. The operator would control the personal archive, access settings and direction of development. Connected AI could search this context, explain connections and assist with ongoing work. [1, pp. 5-6; 3, pp. 4-11]

Aura's boundary is "clothing, not skin": an external, removable system. Its operator would be able to stop using it and delete their personal archive.

Returning to a project after several months could bring back its goals, sources, decisions, results and subsequent changes. The operator could follow the reasoning behind an earlier decision and update the record as the work develops.

Learning and digital hygiene

Aura could help its operator learn quickly, compare sources, question assumptions and test explanations. Digital hygiene would include recognising deceptive or manipulative material, reviewing incoming connections and controlling sharing. These tools would support independent judgement when encountering advertising, political messaging, synthetic media or automated accounts.

Digital twins

A digital twin is a computer-based representation linked to a person, place or activity. A personal twin might combine a measured body model with records of experience. A place twin might represent a room, garden or public path. Records identify measured observations, personal accounts, interpretations and simulations.

The spatial interface

A horn torus is a surface that narrows to a central point rather than leaving an open central hole. Aura's spatial design draws on aura and chakra imagery. Seven nested horn tori organise information around a person and meet at a central infinity point. Personal data occupies the internal faces of the hollow geometry, with additional privacy conditions. Public data occupies the external surfaces. Records receive addresses within this structure and can be explored through spatial views, lists, text or voice. [8; 11]

The operator only shares information or content that is useful to the situation and does not cause them a privacy concern.

Planning an Aura around your life

Home L0 means the person and their controlled private environment, with devices, storage and services arranged to suit their requirements. An everyday Aura might use an existing phone, home equipment, a trusted service or a combination.

An everyday Aura would be planned with the person using it. Their brief would describe the activities, privacy requirements, trusted devices and services, budget and support.

Decision to exploreQuestions that help
Purpose and interactionWhich personal, work or community activities would you like help with? Text, voice, pictures or spatial views? When is initiative welcome?
Ownership and continuityWhich records, configurations and model adaptations would you own and be able to take with you? What depends on a provider's licence or service?
Privacy prioritiesWhich information feels private or secret? Which uses feel acceptable? How do convenience, capability, effort and cost weigh against privacy?
Trust and locationWhich devices, people, providers and locations do you trust for particular jobs? What evidence would help you decide?
Practical lifeWhat equipment and records exist already? How do travel, household life, access needs and connectivity affect the arrangement?
Support and changeWho maintains things? What help is welcome? How would you pause, simplify, move or end the arrangement?

Example brief

"I want help finding my earlier writing, learning music and following local planning proposals. I prefer my existing phone and a service I trust to process selected writing. I want an export I can use elsewhere and a monthly spending limit."

An equipment or service proposal would show where information travels, who can access it, setup and running costs, maintenance and support. The person could compare alternatives against their brief before purchasing equipment or connecting a service. [4, p. 40]

Where would the work happen?

An Aura could use on-device processing, home equipment, online services or a combination. The arrangement would identify where records are stored, where applications process them and how backups are recovered.

For example, online tools could process project documents while family recordings remain on home storage and a dedicated device holds secret material. A managed service could provide storage, processing and backup under an agreement covering operator access, retention and migration.

Browser-based personal tools would need usable export and recovery because storage quotas and automatic clearing can affect retained records. [T5]

Proposal pages 9–11

Public, private and secret information

Public, private and secret views would organise information according to the operator's privacy requirements. Access settings would apply to applications, people and outward releases. [2, pp. 29-31]

ViewA possible exampleChoices to record
Public / outwardAn approved biography, qualification, proposal or contribution statement.Exact wording or media, audience, purpose, date and correction route.
PrivateProject history, personal reflections, selected health records or relationship context.Which functions and people receive access, where processing occurs and for how long.
SecretAn especially intimate account, closely held recollection or other sensitive material.More closely bounded interpretation, training and disclosure; separate protection where chosen.

Permissions would name the material, use, recipient or process, location and duration. Storage, interpretation, model training, external actions and publication would have their own access settings.

Transcripts, summaries, search representations and trained models may retain information from their sources. They would inherit the source restrictions. Public-facing applications would receive approved outward records through controlled access.

Accounts, encryption keys, separate stores and application controls would implement access restrictions. The agreement would identify key holders, applications with readable access, and support or household accounts. [T1]

The design gives governments and technology companies no built-in access to personal archives. Release to a public archive or research trial would be a separate, informed action by the operator, under stated conditions.

The operator would be able to delete their personal archive, backups and derived personal models. Public and research releases would separately state how already shared material is retained, withdrawn or deleted.

Services, processing and recovery

A provider might hold encrypted backups while another service processes selected records. The personal plan would identify the information each service receives, who can read it and what copies it retains.

JobChoices to make visible
Working storageThe main records and copies, their locations, protection, availability and authority when versions differ.
Interpretation and searchWhich records go to the chosen processor, in what form, for which job and with what access.
Personal model trainingThe selected dataset, model, processing location, retained inputs, outputs and provider reuse terms.
Backup and recoveryWhere copies live, who holds keys, who helps after loss and how a restore is demonstrated.
Support and maintenanceWho administers the equipment or account; what support access exists while it is unlocked.
Chosen disclosureThe named recipient, selected representation, purpose, retention and correction or withdrawal route.

Encryption protects information in transit and in storage. Processing may require readable information, so the plan would also identify the processing environment, administrator access and retained logs or copies.

Checks would include service terms, software inspection where available, a demonstration of data transfers and permissions, an export in a usable format and recovery from a backup.

Revoking permission would block pending transfers and future access. For delivered copies, the release record would identify the recipient's retention, correction and deletion process. [2, pp. 27-31]

Extra privacy options

Stronger privacy arrangements would be designed around the information to protect, the likely sources of access and the person's maintenance preferences. Options include app permissions, dedicated devices, local networks and physically disconnected workspaces.

OptionWhat it changesWhat remains to consider
App permissionsLimits access to files, sensors and connected accounts.Permission coverage, provider telemetry and onward processing.
Dedicated device or profileSeparates accounts, app data and device use.Operating-system support, administrators, backups and recovery.
No active SIM/eSIM; home Wi-FiRemoves the ordinary mobile-data connection.Internet-enabled Wi-Fi still permits uploads.
Local-only home networkConnects approved local devices while blocking internet routes for that network.Other connections, local relays, name lookups and what happens on reconnection.
Physically disconnected workspaceSeparates the device from online and household-network services during secret work.Controlled imports, updates, backups, physical access and reviewed exports.

A local-only network would block outgoing internet routes for both IPv4 and IPv6, the two common internet addressing systems. Configuration would also cover permitted local services, devices that can forward information and automatic synchronisation. [T1; T7]

Apple documents app sandboxing, which separates apps' private files, and a local-network permission distinct from internet access. On compatible hardware, GrapheneOS offers user profiles and a per-app Network permission covering local networks too. [T2-T4]

Apps may queue uploads for reconnection. An offline setup would include a checked import, update and reconnection process. [T6]

Proposal pages 14–15

The separate Aura Genesis programme

Genesis is a proposed, separate opt-in programme for developing an ultra-high-definition Aura twin and learning to work with it through twin socialising. It is not required for Verse Box, an everyday Aura, C-Hours or civic participation. [2, pp. 1, 15]

Biosensors chosen by the participant

Participants choose which compatible biosensors to use and how many. These map the quantified self: measured biological signals that help the twin reflect its operator's biology. The sensors are external and removable.

Options include scalp EEG for brain electrical activity, ECG or optical pulse, breathing sensors, oxygen saturation, blood pressure, skin conductance, temperature, eye tracking, movement and surface EMG for muscle activity. The full chosen suite would be visible in the participation agreement. [2, pp. 17-18]

Sessions and twin socialising

The proposed pressure programme is 60 two-hour sessions, totalling 120 hours. Cameras, microphones and all installed biosensors would record throughout each defined session, including rest. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) uses oxygen in a pressurised chamber; clinical assessment, equipment and operating conditions would have a participant-specific plan. [2, pp. 1-3; T8]

Afterwards, twin socialising would practise memory, timing, initiative, correction and approved tool use in everyday life. Recording outside pressure sessions would have its own start and stop permissions. [2, pp. 15, 34]

Research and outward release

Truth Engine research would examine patterns around truthful answers, instructed falsehoods, uncertainty and changed understanding. Its methods and results would require evaluation. Research participation and release of personal data would each have their own agreement.

A public or research release would identify the exact information, recipient, purpose, retention and withdrawal conditions before the operator commits it. The personal archive would remain under the operator's control, with removal and deletion choices. [2, pp. 29-31]

Storage and software for the complete record

Genesis storage would include the complete chosen biosensor suite, cameras and microphones throughout pressure sessions, alongside existing records, models and backups. The number of sensors, recording rates, duration and retention determine the capacity required. [2, pp. 25-28]

The proposed Genesis application would be developed and validated to Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) standards, with controlled acquisition, secure storage and tested recovery. [T9]

Developing a personal AI

A language model is trained software that works with language. Large language model is commonly shortened to LLM. Aura's proposed nanoLLM would be a compact personal model adapted to its operator's tasks, preferences and available computing.

Build the personal dataset

The dataset would bring together source records, context, examples of desired responses and the operator's corrections. Everyday use would reveal missing information and recurring errors. Reviewing those cases would develop the material for personalisation and possible training.

StageWork involved
Define the taskDescribe the intended result, representative situations and criteria for a satisfactory response.
Prepare sourcesLink records to their dates, origins and access conditions; retain the operator's context and corrections.
Use and correctReview responses, record errors and add examples of the operator's preferred response.
Review coverageCheck missing context, contradictions and variation across situations.
Consider trainingIf retrieval is insufficient, assess adaptation methods, training examples, computing needs, cost and access settings.
EvaluateTest on examples withheld from training and compare performance with the current arrangement, including uncertainty and privacy behaviour.

Retrieval and training

Retrieval supplies relevant records to a model when it handles a task. Training changes the model using approved examples. Retrieval could provide personal context before adaptation is needed. Models trained on private or secret sources would inherit their access restrictions. [2, pp. 27-31]

The model record would include its task, training sources, processing location, evaluation results and known limits.
