01 / Community Hours

An hour of human contribution.

One C-Hour recognises one verified hour of voluntary human contribution. It has no monetary conversion.

Everyday care, informal help, mentoring and organised activities could all contribute. The proposed record would preserve what happened, what the person learned and who benefited.

An Aura could prepare recognition from conversation, agreed activity details and personally chosen sources. A host, peer or recipient could help confirm an account. Corrections would happen through the same conversation.

Community chooses its thanks.

Communities would shape their own recognition, shared experiences and reciprocal opportunities. C-Hours carry no monetary value or financial entitlement. Paid work, money, contribution and outcomes remain separate records.

02 / An illustrative example

Making a gathering more accessible.

In the proposal’s fictional example, a resident notices that a route to a gathering is difficult for people using mobility aids. Their Aura helps describe the issue, find the responsible organisation and compare immediate and longer-term changes.

A volunteer gives 80 minutes to preparing the gathering: 1⅓ C-Hours. An agreed account could recognise that contribution while the shared project follows the access request, response and observed outcome.

03 / P4A

Follow an issue through to its outcome.

Purple Party for Australia (P4A) proposes an interface for understanding public issues, comparing possible changes and following responsible organisations and decisions.

  1. Describe the issue, place and people affected.
  2. Connect dated sources, alternatives, assumptions and uncertainty.
  3. Identify the responsible body, process, eligibility and dates.
  4. Record proposals, objections, amendments and submissions.
  5. Follow the decision, reasons, delivery and observed outcome.
04 / A fair go

Joyful Responsible Abundance.

Joyful means room for relationships, curiosity, creativity, rest and enjoyment. Responsible means care for consequences, including who benefits, who carries costs and effects on the environment. Abundance means wider access to knowledge, resources, care and opportunity.

The wider proposal connects learning, mentoring, cooperative ownership and shared computing. These are development directions whose practical arrangements would be agreed locally.
